About Me

I'm Ioannis Miltiadou, a QA Automation Engineer based in Pafos, Cyprus, with over 4 years of professional experience building robust test automation solutions. I hold a BSc in Applied Informatics and Computer Science from the University of Macedonia, where I developed a strong foundation in software development and problem-solving.

My professional journey began with a Cloud Engineering internship at Deloitte Greece, Since joining Amdocs in 2021, I've progressed from Junior Test Engineer to Software Development Engineer in Test, where I specialized in developing automation solutions using Java, working with Jenkins and Git, and building data visualization dashboards with Grafana. Currently, I'm a QA Automation Engineer at Woodlaine Digital Solutions, continuing to design scalable automation frameworks and integrate CI/CD pipelines.

Beyond my technical work, I'm deeply passionate about football analytics. I'm currently pursuing an MSc in Data Analytics in Football at Sports Data Campus, combining my software engineering expertise with tactical analysis. I've earned multiple certifications in performance analysis, data analytics, video analysis, and talent identification from organizations like INSA, PFSA, and AFCAS. My goal is to bridge the gap between technical problem-solving and tactical insights, creating data-driven visualizations and tools that make football analysis actionable and accessible.

I'm fluent in Greek and English, with beginner-level Spanish. When I'm not coding or analyzing football data, I enjoy working on personal projects that range from D&D tools to utility applications, always looking for creative ways to solve problems through technology.
